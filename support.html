Support

Questions, problems or something to report? Email support.competitions@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you, usually within a day or two.

Common questions

My steps aren’t showing up

Check that Competitions is allowed to read steps: open the app, go to Me → Step tracking. On iPhone you can also check Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Competitions; on Android, open Health Connect → App permissions. Steps sync when you open the app, when you pull down to refresh, and about every hour in the background.

How do I invite friends?

Open a challenge and tap Invite friends. Share the link with anyone, or pick people you’ve done challenges with before.

When does a challenge end?

At 11:59 pm on the end date in the organiser’s time zone. Results are final 24 hours later, so everyone’s phone has time to sync the last day.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and enter your email. Open the link in the email on your phone to choose a new password.

How do I report or block someone?

Long-press their chat message, choose what’s wrong and tap Send report. You can also block them from the same screen. Blocked people are listed in Me → Blocked people, where you can unblock them. You can also email us about anything that worries you.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

In the app, go to Me → Notifications to choose which alerts you get.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Me → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and data, as described in the Privacy Policy. Challenges you organised are deleted for everyone in them.
