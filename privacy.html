Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

Competitions is a walking challenge app run by Stefan D’Mello in Australia (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

The short version
  • We collect only what the app needs to run challenges between you and the people you invite.
  • Your step counts are shared only with people in challenges you join.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your information.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time in the app: Me → Delete account.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Email address and password, or your Apple or Google sign-inTo create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash; we can’t see them. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address (or an Apple private relay address if you choose Hide My Email) and the name you share, which we offer as your display name. We never see your Apple or Google password.
Display name and optional profile photoSo friends can recognise you on leaderboards, invites and chat.
Daily step totalsTo score the challenges you take part in. With your permission the app reads daily step counts from Apple Health, Health Connect or your phone’s motion sensor. Steps can’t be entered by hand. We store one total per day and where it came from (for example “Apple Health”). We don’t read any other health data.
Challenges you create or joinNames, descriptions, dates, goals and your time zone (to end challenges at midnight where the organiser lives), plus who is taking part and who was invited.
Chat messagesTo show messages to the other people in that challenge.
Reports and blocksIf you report a message, we keep the report, a copy of the message and the reason so we can review it. If you block someone, we keep that so the block keeps working.
Notifications and settingsYour notification history and preferences, and a push token for each device you sign in on, so we can send alerts you’ve asked for.

We don’t collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or analytics about how you use the app.

Who can see your information

  • People in your challenges can see your display name, photo, daily step totals for that challenge, your rank and your chat messages in that challenge.
  • People you’ve shared a challenge with can see your name and photo (for example in their Friends list), and can invite you to other challenges.
  • Anyone with a challenge’s invite link who signs in can see its name, dates, goal, organiser and the names of the people taking part, so they can decide whether to join.
  • Profile photos are stored at a hard-to-guess web address so the app can display them. Anyone who has that exact address could view the photo.
  • Other people never see your email address.

Health information

Step counts may be health information. We use them only to run your challenges. We never use step data for advertising or marketing, never sell it, and never share it with anyone other than the people in your challenges and the service providers below. Data from Apple HealthKit and Health Connect is used only as described here. You can turn off step access at any time in your phone’s settings (Health or Health Connect) or in the app under Me → Step tracking.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app. They process information only on our behalf:

  • Supabase hosts our database, accounts, file storage and sign-in emails. Your data is stored in Sydney, Australia.
  • Expo (United States) delivers push notifications to your device. It receives your device’s push token and the text of each notification.
  • Apple and Google deliver push notifications to iPhones and Android phones, provide Apple Health and Health Connect on your device, and confirm who you are if you choose Sign in with Apple or Google.

Because Expo, Apple and Google may process notification content outside Australia (including in the United States), some information is disclosed overseas when we send you a notification. We only use providers that commit to protecting it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photo, step data, challenges you organised (for everyone in them), notifications and devices, and we remove the text of your chat messages. Reports about content may be kept for as long as we need them to deal with the report or keep the app safe. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a short time before it’s overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can see and edit your profile in the app (Me → Edit profile) and change notification settings at any time.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app (Me → Delete account).
  • Other requests: email us at support.competitions@gmail.com to ask for a copy of your information or anything else about your privacy. We’ll respond within 30 days.
  • Complaints: contact us first and we’ll try to resolve it. If you’re not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

If you live outside Australia, you may have additional rights under your local law (for example in the EU or UK). Contact us and we’ll help.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit, access is limited by database rules so people can only read what they’re allowed to, and every change goes through checks on our server. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don’t use elsewhere.

Children

Competitions is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
