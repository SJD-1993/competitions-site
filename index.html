Get your mates moving.

Competitions is an app for walking challenges with friends. Start a challenge, invite people with a link, and race to the top of the leaderboard as your steps sync from your phone.

  • Pick dates and a goal: most steps wins, or everyone who hits a target.
  • Live leaderboard, daily progress and results.
  • Chat with everyone taking part.
